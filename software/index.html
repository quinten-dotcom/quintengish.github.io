Questions

Good questions.

Do I need to install anything?

No. You add UM Studio as a connector inside Claude or ChatGPT and sign in. That is it.

Which AI plan do I need?

A Claude plan that supports connectors, or a ChatGPT plan that supports apps and connectors.

How long does a video take?

Most videos take a few minutes. Your AI tells you when yours is ready.

Do I get the templates?

No. The looks run on our side and you get the finished video. You own the videos you make.

What does it cost?

Free accounts get 3 videos in total, each up to 30 seconds. Pro is $20 a month for 30 videos a month, with no 30 second limit. You can cancel any time from your account page.

How do I sign in?

Click Get free access and sign in with Google on software.quintengish.com. No password needed. Your account page shows your plan and your newest videos.
